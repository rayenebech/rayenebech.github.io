Selected work

Tietoevry, for HUS Helsinki University Hospital

Real-time speech translation between clinicians and patients

Doctors and patients who don't share a language usually wait for an interpreter. I contributed to a live speech-to-speech pipeline that lets them talk directly: speech is transcribed as it's spoken, translated with medical context, and voiced back in the patient's language.

I co-authored the academic evaluation of the system, and my master's thesis compared this cascaded design against direct speech-to-speech models.

  • Streaming STT
  • Machine translation
  • TTS
  • Azure Speech
  • Healthcare

Tietoevry

Multi-agent assistant for enterprise customer enquiries

Customer questions often need answers drawn from several internal knowledge bases at once. I architected a multi-agent RAG system in which an orchestrator routes each enquiry to specialised agents for retrieval and tool use, then composes a grounded reply with its sources.

  • Multi-agent orchestration
  • RAG
  • Tool use
  • Voicebot

KUIS AI Lab, Koç University, open source

Hippocrates: open medical language models

Most strong medical LLMs are closed, which makes them hard to study or trust. With the KUIS AI Lab team I co-developed Hippocrates, an open framework covering the full training recipe, from domain pre-training to preference alignment.

The resulting models, built on Mistral and LLaMA-2, reached state-of-the-art results on medical benchmarks among open models of their size.

  • Continual pre-training
  • Instruction tuning
  • RLHF / RLAIF
  • Open source

New Mind, Istanbul

LLM legal assistant with built-in safeguards

Lawyers need answers that point back to the source and can't be steered off course. I led an assistant that retrieves from legal documents, answers with a LoRA-tuned model, and screens every request for prompt injection and jailbreak attempts first.

Fine-tuned with distributed training, quantized for cheaper serving, and deployed behind FastAPI.

  • LoRA
  • Quantization
  • RAG
  • Guardrails
  • FastAPI
